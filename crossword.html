      How to Play

      Click a cell to select it (click again to switch Across/Down). Type letters to fill the grid - the cursor moves automatically. Use the clue list to jump to any word. Use Check to verify your answers, Reveal Letter for a hint, Clear to restart the board, or New Puzzle to switch to a different themed word set. There are 4 rotating themes: Wiring Harness Basics, Lear and PETC Company, Programs and Acronyms, and Process and Documentation - a new one is featured each day!
