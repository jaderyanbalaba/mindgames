    How to Play

    Click and drag from the first letter of a word to its last letter (words can go across, down, or diagonally, forwards or backwards). Release to check it - correct words turn green and get crossed off the list. Use Reveal Word if you get stuck, Clear to reset your progress, or New Puzzle to switch to a different themed word set. There are 4 rotating themes: Wiring Harness Basics, Lear and PETC Company, Programs and Acronyms, and Process and Documentation - a new one is featured each day!
